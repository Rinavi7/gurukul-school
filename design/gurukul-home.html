An old idea of learning, renewed

In a gurukul, students learned by living close to their teachers, asking questions and working with their hands. We keep that closeness: small classes, mentors who know every child, and a timetable that leaves room to build, perform and play.

The learning journey

Four stages, each building on the last, from play-based foundations to board exams and career guidance.

  1. 1

    Foundation

    Nursery to Class 2

    Learning through play, stories, rhythm and early numbers.

  2. 2

    Preparatory

    Classes 3 to 5

    Reading, maths and science through hands-on activities.

  3. 3

    Middle

    Classes 6 to 8

    Subjects deepen; coding and lab projects begin.

  4. 4

    Secondary

    Classes 9 to 12

    Board preparation, stream choice and career guidance.
